Ridgeline - setting up memberships, feedback and first responder keys

All settings are at the top of the script in index.html, in two blocks:
MEMBERSHIP (plans and payments) and CONTACT (where messages go).
Open index.html in any text editor, search for "MEMBERSHIP SETTINGS", edit, save, and upload again.

PLANS AND PRICES (already filled in)
  Free   - maps, GPS, weather, up to 10 places, all search and rescue tools
  Basic  - $4.99/month or $39.99/year: unlimited places, routes, tracks, hunting areas,
           custom team icons, QR codes, GPX/GeoJSON files
  Pro    - $9.99/month or $79.99/year: everything in Basic plus offline maps, 3D,
           elevation and steepness, projected routes
  Search and rescue safety tools (check-ins, last known point, clues, sectors, teams)
  are always free so nobody is blocked during a search.

OPTION A - LEMON SQUEEZY (recommended: handles sales tax for you)
  1. Create a store at lemonsqueezy.com.
  2. Create a product named "Ridgeline Basic" with two subscription variants: Monthly $4.99 and Yearly $39.99.
  3. Create a product named "Ridgeline Pro" with Monthly $9.99 and Yearly $79.99.
     The word "Pro" must be in the product or variant name; that is how the app knows the plan.
  4. Turn on "Generate license keys" for both products. Buyers get the key in their receipt email.
  5. Copy each variant's checkout link (Share > Checkout URL) into MEMBERSHIP.checkout:
       basic_month, basic_year, pro_month, pro_year
  6. Set provider:'lemonsqueezy'.

OPTION B - GUMROAD
  1. Create membership products for Basic and Pro (monthly and yearly tiers) and turn on license keys.
  2. Paste the checkout links into MEMBERSHIP.checkout.
  3. Put each product id in gumroadProducts, e.g. gumroadProducts:{'abc123':'basic','def456':'pro'}.
  4. Set provider:'gumroad'.

STRIPE
  Stripe does not give license keys that an app can check on its own. It needs a small server
  that records who paid. Ask for this if you want Stripe later.

FIRST RESPONDER KEYS (free Pro for verified responders)
  1. Lemon Squeezy: create a product named "Ridgeline First Responder" priced at $0,
     with license keys turned on, and hide it from your store.
     Gumroad: create a $0 product and add it as gumroadProducts:{'<id>':'responder'}.
  2. When a verification request arrives, check it: reply to the agency email address they gave
     (not a personal one), or call the agency. Never ask for photos of ID cards.
  3. Once confirmed, send them the $0 checkout link. Their receipt email contains the key.
     They enter it under Menu > See plans > Already paid.
  Responder keys unlock Pro plus responder mode (USNG grid, 24-hour times, incident log).

WHERE FEEDBACK AND VERIFICATION REQUESTS GO (CONTACT block)
  - formUrl: create a free form at formspree.io (or a similar service) and paste its endpoint,
    e.g. formUrl:'https://formspree.io/f/xxxxxxx'. Messages arrive in your email.
    Messages written without a connection are sent automatically later.
  - email: an address people are shown if no formUrl is set (they copy and send it themselves).
  - On the shared claude.ai page, messages are saved in that page's database instead.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Without internet, a paid plan keeps working for 30 days, then needs one check online.
  - The plan check runs inside the app. Someone technical could get around it.
    That is normal for apps without a server and is fine for a small business.
  - You need a privacy policy and terms of sale before charging. Lemon Squeezy and Gumroad act
    as the seller of record and cover payment terms, but your app still needs a privacy policy.
  - Testing: set paywall:false to turn everything on while you check things.
